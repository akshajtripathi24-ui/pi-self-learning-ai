🔬 What happens when an AI learns from its own writing?

Round 0: a new tiny GPT learns to write from 100 real space and robot facts. Round 1: another new one learns only from sentences round 0 wrote. Round 2 learns only from round 1… Nothing checks the sentences. Watch what happens!

Each round takes a few minutes on a Pi. You can close the page; it keeps going.

What to watch

Different words used: how big its vocabulary still is. Collapse makes it shrink.

Different sentences: how much it repeats itself. Lower = more repetition.

Real words: how many of its words actually exist in the real facts.

Surprise at the real facts: how strange the real sentences look to it. Higher = it has forgotten what real writing is like.

Then run it again with keep the real facts ticked and compare. That's why AI companies need fresh human writing, and why AI needs a checker.
